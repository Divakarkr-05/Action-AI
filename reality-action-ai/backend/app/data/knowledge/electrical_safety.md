# Electrical Safety & De-Energization Standards

## Core Principle: De-Energize and Lock Out
Never work on live electrical equipment unless de-energization introduces additional hazards or is physically infeasible. Always perform Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) before opening enclosures, touching terminals, or replacing components.

## Verification of De-Energized State (Test-Before-Touch)
1. Select an appropriately rated digital multimeter (CAT III 600V or CAT IV 600V minimum for industrial/mains applications).
2. Test the multimeter on a known live voltage source to verify meter and test lead integrity.
3. Test all phase-to-phase, phase-to-neutral, and phase-to-ground conductor combinations on the target circuit.
4. Re-test the multimeter on the known live source to verify the meter did not fail during the test.

## Arc Flash & Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Always wear safety glasses with side shields, non-flammable arc-rated clothing, and voltage-rated insulated gloves when inspecting or servicing electrical panels.
- Maintain safe approach boundaries: keep unqualified personnel outside the restricted boundary (typically 12 inches for 50V–750V systems).
- Inspect test leads for cracked insulation, exposed copper, or loose probe tips prior to every measurement.

## Emergency Response
If electrical contact or electric shock occurs:
- Do NOT touch the victim directly if they may still be in contact with the live source.
- Immediately de-energize the circuit via the nearest emergency stop, disconnect switch, or circuit breaker.
- Use a non-conductive rescue hook or insulated object to separate the victim from the conductor.
- Call emergency medical services immediately and begin CPR/AED if trained and indicated.
